Our Mission

Good food belongs on plates, not in bins.

Every year, tonnes of perfectly edible food are thrown away while people in the same communities go without. FoodLoop was built to close that gap — a simple platform where restaurants, supermarkets, vendors and individuals can list surplus food, and neighbours can reserve and collect it before it's wasted.

We believe rescuing food should be as easy as ordering it. No complicated logistics, no middlemen — just local people helping local people.

Our Story

It started with one unsold meal too many.

FoodLoop began after watching a local bakery throw away dozens of fresh loaves at closing time — while students a street away were skipping meals to save money.

We realised the problem wasn't a lack of food, or a lack of need. It was a lack of connection. So we built FoodLoop: a marketplace where surplus food finds a home in minutes.

Today, hundreds of providers list food daily, and thousands of rescuers pick it up — good food, fair prices, zero waste.
