How FoodLoop Works

A simple way to connect surplus food with people who can use it.

1

List Food

Restaurants, vendors and individuals list their available surplus food.

2

Find Food

People browse available surplus food in their area.

3

Reserve

Users reserve the food they want before it becomes unavailable.

4

Rescue

The food is collected and enjoyed instead of being thrown away.

Why FoodLoop?

Let's give surplus food another destination.

Every day, businesses and individuals have food that is still perfectly good but no longer needed. FoodLoop gives that food a second chance to be discovered, reserved and rescued.
